Lion

The African lion (Panthera leo) is a majestic apex predator that plays a crucial role in maintaining the balance of ecosystems across the sub-Saharan savannahs. Known as the "King of the Jungle," these magnificent big cats actually live in grasslands and plains rather than dense rainforests. They are the only truly social felines, forming structured groups called prides that consist of related females, their offspring, and a small coalition of dominant males. While the heavy-maned males are responsible for defending the territory from rivals, the smaller and more agile lionesses work together as a highly coordinated team to hunt large prey like zebras, wildebeests, and buffaloes.
